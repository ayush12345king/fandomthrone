Refunds and cancellations

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

What you buy

Each payment buys an instant, digital service: your amount is added to the total of the name you back on a public leaderboard, with your display name and optional message. There is no prize, reward or physical product.

When we refund

  • You were charged but your shoutout never appeared on the board.
  • You were charged twice for the same shoutout.
  • We removed your shoutout because of a mistake on our side.

Email ayushbathamachhalda@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with your payment ID. Approved refunds are made through Dodo Payments, our reseller, back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days.

When we don't refund

Once a shoutout appears on the board, the service has been delivered, so it can't be cancelled. Being overtaken by other fans, changing your mind, or a shoutout removed for breaking our Terms are not grounds for a refund.

Cancellations

You can cancel any time before you complete payment. Closing the payment window means nothing is charged.
