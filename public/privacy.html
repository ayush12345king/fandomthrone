What we store

For each shoutout we store the board, the name you backed, the amount, the display name you typed and your optional message. These are public on the site. There are no accounts and no passwords.

Payments

Your payment is handled by Dodo Payments, our reseller and Merchant of Record. They collect your card or UPI details, email and billing country under their own privacy policy. We receive a payment reference, not your card number.

On your device

Your browser remembers the last display name you used so you don't have to type it again. We don't use advertising cookies or trackers.
